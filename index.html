The Pramukh story

A heritage in gold, built for institutional trust.

From wholesale gold ornaments to professional gold valuation and banking support.

Pramukh Jewellers began in 1990 with wholesale gold ornaments, developing deep practical knowledge of gold, jewellery and the realities of the market.

Around 2012, the business evolved into a sole-proprietorship firm and expanded its focus toward professional gold valuation services and institutional work.

Today, the firm supports activities connected with gold loans, audits, bank gold auctions and related valuation requirements.

“Experience in gold. Discipline in valuation. Trust in every relationship.”
